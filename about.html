Our story

Self-care, done properly.

Elysera started with a simple complaint: the prettiest bubble baths left our skin tight and dry. Glitter, dyes and strong foaming agents make a beautiful tub and an unhappy skin barrier.

So we built the bath we wanted. A gentle base that still makes deep, lasting foam, with ceramides and niacinamide added the way a skincare brand would add them. Then we gave each scent a place: the mountain air of Saint Moritz, the vanilla of Zanzibar, Kyoto in blossom season.

Elysera is made in British Columbia and run from Vancouver by Lindsay and Marc. Bubble bath is where we begin. Robes, candles and home fragrance will follow, held to the same standard.

What we hold to

Four rules for everything we make.

  • Skin firstEvery formula supports the skin barrier. If an ingredient only makes the bath look good, it doesn't go in.
  • Short ingredient listsEach one listed in full, in English and French, including fragrance allergens.
  • Made in CanadaFormulated and filled in British Columbia, in a licensed cosmetic facility.
  • Gift-readyPackaging you can hand over without wrapping, and a handwritten card on request.
