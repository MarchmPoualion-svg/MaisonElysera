The formula

Most bubble baths strip your skin. Ours is built to protect it.

Foaming agents in ordinary bubble baths pull oils from the skin's outer layer. Elysera uses a mild, plant-derived cleansing base and adds the same barrier ingredients found in serious skincare.

  • CeramidesLipids that make up much of the skin barrier. They help skin hold its moisture while you soak.
  • NiacinamideVitamin B3. Supports the barrier and helps calm skin that feels tight after bathing.
  • Gentle cleansersMild surfactants that foam well without the squeaky, stripped feeling.
  • glitter and microplastics
  • artificial dyes
  • harsh sulfates

The ritual

Twenty minutes, somewhere else.

  1. Pour

    Add two capfuls under warm running water. The bubbles build as the bath fills.

  2. Soak

    Stay fifteen to twenty minutes. Warm, not hot, water is kinder to the skin barrier.

  3. Seal

    Pat dry and moisturise within three minutes, while skin is still slightly damp.

Questions

Bubble bath, answered.

Is bubble bath bad for your skin?

Many bubble baths use strong foaming agents, glitter and dyes that can leave skin feeling dry or tight after a soak. Elysera bubble bath uses mild cleansers and adds ceramides and niacinamide to help skin hold its moisture while you bathe.

Is Elysera bubble bath suitable for sensitive skin?

Elysera is made without glitter, dyes or harsh sulfates, for skin that often feels dry or tight after bathing. If your skin reacts easily, check the full ingredient list and patch-test before your first bath.

How much bubble bath should I use?

Pour two capfuls, about 30 ml, under warm running water. The bubbles build as the bath fills. A 400 ml bottle makes about twelve baths.

Does Elysera bubble bath contain glitter, dye or microplastics?

No. Elysera bubble bath contains no glitter, no microplastics and no artificial dyes. The colour of each bath comes from the bubbles, not from additives.
